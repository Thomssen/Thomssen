PSYCHOSEC - privacy summary

* Your investigations (targets, notes, findings, files, reports) stay on this computer in an encrypted local database. They are never sent to PSYCHOSEC servers.
* To verify access, PSYCHOSEC's service records your Discord identity, your sign-in IP address and time, session activity and the application version.
* This is used only to protect the service and administer access. Records are deleted automatically after a fixed retention period.
* Lookups you run are sent directly from your computer to the public services needed to answer them.

Full policy: https://psychosec.org/privacy
