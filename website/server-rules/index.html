Discord Server Rules

These rules apply to everyone in the official Thomsen Software Discord community — the server for Thomsen Shield, Thomsen OSINT, Thomsen VM, and the Thomsen Protection bot. Read them before you post, and follow staff guidance if anything here is unclear.

  1. 01

    Respect Everyone

    Treat all members with respect. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and targeted abuse are not tolerated.

  2. 02

    No Impersonation

    Don't impersonate staff, other members, or Thomsen Software itself, and don't use a misleading name or avatar to pass yourself off as someone you're not.

  3. 03

    No Spam or Raids

    Don't spam messages, flood channels, raid the server, or otherwise disrupt conversations on purpose.

  4. 04

    No Scams or Malicious Links

    Phishing attempts, malware, suspicious files, fraudulent offers, and malicious links are prohibited.

  5. 05

    Use the Correct Channels

    Keep conversations in the channels meant for them, and follow each channel's individual purpose — including using support channels or tickets for individual help instead of general chat.

  6. 06

    Product Discussion & Bug Reports

    Feedback and bug reports on Thomsen Shield, Thomsen OSINT, Thomsen VM, and the Thomsen Protection bot are welcome. Use the channels set up for them, include enough detail for staff to act on, and avoid posting sensitive information (license keys, tokens, personal data) in a public channel.

  7. 07

    No Unauthorized Advertising

    Don't advertise products, services, servers, or other communities without permission from staff, and don't send unsolicited promotional DMs to other members.

  8. 08

    Protect Privacy

    Don't share private information or private conversations belonging to yourself or others without their consent.

  9. 09

    No Malicious Activity

    Don't use the community to distribute malicious tools, coordinate attacks, or facilitate unauthorized access to systems.

  10. 10

    Follow Discord's Rules

    All members must follow Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

  11. 11

    Respect Staff Decisions

    Follow reasonable moderator instructions. If you disagree with a decision, raise it with staff through the appropriate channel.

  12. 12

    Use Common Sense

    Behavior that seriously disrupts the community may still be moderated, even if it isn't explicitly listed above.
