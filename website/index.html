How it works

Three things, done well.

Thomsen Shield doesn't try to do everything. It watches your connections, flags what looks wrong, and gives you full control over Windows Firewall — nothing more, nothing hidden.

01

Monitor

See every active connection on your PC — the app behind it, the remote address, and its status — updated live.

02

Detect

Connection spikes and repeated connections from one IP are flagged automatically, with a sensitivity level you control.

03

Control

Block an IP or app in one click, or manage Windows Firewall rules directly — Thomsen Shield only ever touches rules it created.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

A full set of network and firewall tools in one deliberately minimal interface. This is Thomsen Shield — see the full product page for compatibility details and the complete feature set.

Dashboard

Protection and firewall status, active connection count, blocked IPs, and a small live activity graph — the whole picture in one deliberately minimal screen. It’s the first thing you see on launch, and every number on it updates in real time, so you never have to dig through separate tabs just to confirm the app is actually watching.

Live Connections

Every active TCP/UDP connection with the owning app, remote address, and status. Block, allow, or copy an IP straight from its details. The list updates live as connections open and close, so what you’re looking at always matches what’s actually happening on the wire right now.

Protection

Automatic detection of connection spikes and repeated connections from one IP, with a Low / Normal / High sensitivity control. It starts watching the moment Thomsen Shield opens, with nothing to configure first, and every flagged connection shows up in Live Connections and the activity log so you can see exactly what tripped it. Need to lock things down fast? Lockdown Mode temporarily raises sensitivity to High and blocks flagged connections automatically until you turn it off.

Firewall

View, create, enable, disable, and remove rules — all prefixed so Thomsen Shield only ever touches rules it created itself. It’s built on top of the Windows Firewall you already have rather than replacing it, so you get a friendlier interface without anything changing about how Windows actually filters your traffic.

Blocklist & Allowlist

Every blocked IP with its reason and expiration. Trusted IPs and private ranges are protected from auto-blocking. Add or remove entries by hand whenever you like, and choose whether a block expires on its own or stays in place until you lift it yourself.

Events & Logs

A readable, filterable activity history — exportable to TXT or JSON whenever you need it. Blocks, rule changes, and detected spikes all land here, so if something happened while you weren’t watching, there’s still a record of exactly what and when.
