How it works

Three things, done well.

Thomsen Shield doesn't try to do everything. It watches your connections, flags what looks wrong, and gives you full control over Windows Firewall — nothing more, nothing hidden.

01

Monitor

See every active connection on your PC — the app behind it, the remote address, and its status — updated live.

02

Detect

Connection spikes and repeated connections from one IP are flagged automatically, with a sensitivity level you control.

03

Control

Block an IP or app in one click, or manage Windows Firewall rules directly — Thomsen Shield only ever touches rules it created.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

A full set of network and firewall tools in one deliberately minimal interface.

Dashboard

Protection and firewall status, active connection count, blocked IPs, and a small live activity graph. Deliberately minimal.

Live Connections

Every active TCP/UDP connection with the owning app, remote address, and status. Block, allow, or copy an IP from its details.

Protection

Automatic detection of connection spikes and repeated connections from one IP, with a Low / Normal / High sensitivity control.

Firewall

View, create, enable, disable, and remove rules — all prefixed so Thomsen Shield only ever touches rules it created itself.

Blocklist & Allowlist

Every blocked IP with its reason and expiration. Trusted IPs and private ranges are protected from auto-blocking.

Events & Logs

A readable, filterable activity history — exportable to TXT or JSON whenever you need it.
