Refund Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

We want every purchase from Thomssen to feel safe. This page explains when a refund is available, what isn't covered, and how to request one - or use the form further down if you'd rather not send an email.

How refunds work

Most purchases are eligible for a full refund if you request one within the window below. Each request is reviewed individually, and approved refunds are returned to your original payment method.

Eligible for a refund

  • Requested within 14 days of the original purchase date
  • Purchased directly through thomssen.dev or an official Thomssen checkout
  • The order ID or license key provided matches an active purchase on file
  • The product or service hasn't been used in a way that breaks the Terms of Service

Not eligible for a refund

  • Requests made after the 14-day window, outside case-by-case exceptions
  • Custom or bespoke work that has already been completed and delivered
  • Licenses or accounts suspended for fraud, chargeback abuse, or a Terms violation
  • Chargebacks filed with your bank before contacting support first

How to request a refund

  1. Fill out the Request a Refund form below, or email support@thomssen.dev directly
  2. Include your order ID or license key so the purchase can be found
  3. You'll get a confirmation and, once reviewed, the outcome - both by email

Expected processing time

  • Requests are reviewed within 2 business days
  • Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method
  • Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider
  • Any associated license or access is deactivated once a refund is issued

Contact & support

Questions before you request a refund? Reach out at support@thomssen.dev and we'll get back to you.
