Refund Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

If Thomsen Shield isn't right for you, this page explains how to get a refund - or use the form further down if you'd rather not send an email.

How refunds work

Email us within 7 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full - no questions asked, no forms. Past that window, refunds are handled case by case, for example if the app has a defect we're unable to fix.

Eligible for a refund

  • Requested within 7 days of the original purchase date
  • Purchased directly through the official Thomsen Shield checkout
  • The order ID or license key provided matches an active purchase on file

Not eligible for a refund

  • Requests made after the 7-day window, outside case-by-case exceptions
  • Licenses suspended for fraud, chargeback abuse, or a Terms of Service violation
  • Chargebacks filed with your bank before contacting support first

How to request a refund

  1. Fill out the Request a Refund form below, or email support@thomssen.dev directly
  2. Include your license key or the email address you purchased with
  3. That's it - no forms, no justification needed within the 7-day window

Expected processing time

  • We refund the full €50.00 to your original payment method through Stripe
  • Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank
  • Your license is deactivated once the refund is issued

Contact & support

Questions before you request a refund? Reach out at support@thomssen.dev and we'll get back to you.
