Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

This page explains what thomssen.dev collects when you visit or buy something here, why, and what your options are. Run by Kristian Thomsen ("we", "us").

The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run this site and fulfill a purchase - basic visit data, and if you buy something, your email and payment details (handled by our payment processor, not stored by us). We don't sell your data.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details - if you email us, use a contact form, or buy something, we get whatever you provide: typically your name and email address.
  • Purchase information - order and license details tied to your email, so we can support you and process refunds.
  • Payment information - handled entirely by our payment processor (e.g. Stripe). We never see or store your full card number.
  • Cookies and similar technology - see the Cookies section below.
  • Standard server logs - our hosting provider logs basic request metadata like IP address, as most web infrastructure does, mainly for security and abuse prevention.

2. Cookies

This site uses a cookie banner to ask for your consent before setting anything beyond strictly necessary cookies. Necessary cookies keep the site working; optional cookies (e.g. analytics) are only set if you allow them, and you can change your choice at any time by revisiting the cookie banner or clearing your browser's site data for thomssen.dev.

3. How we use it

To operate this site, respond to you, process a purchase and its payment, prevent fraud (e.g. chargebacks, key sharing), and understand how the site is used so we can improve it. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

Only the services that make this site and its products work - for example a payment processor for purchases, and the infrastructure that hosts this site. Each processes data under their own privacy policy, and only as needed to provide their service to us.

5. Data retention

We keep purchase and support records for as long as needed to provide support and honor a license, plus whatever period is required for tax and accounting records afterward.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing support@thomssen.dev. Note that deleting your data may mean we can no longer support an active license tied to it.

7. Security

Data in transit to and from this site is encrypted (TLS). Access to systems holding customer data is limited to what's needed to operate the site and support customers.

8. Children

This site isn't directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
