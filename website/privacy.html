Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

Thomsen Shield is a network security tool, so we know privacy matters more than usual here. This page explains exactly what we collect and, just as importantly, what we don't.

The short version: Thomsen Shield's network monitoring and blocking runs entirely on your PC. Your connection activity, blocked IPs, and firewall rules are never uploaded to us. We only handle what's needed to sell you a license and keep it working: your email, a device identifier, and payment details handled by Stripe.

1. What we collect

  • Email address - collected by Stripe at checkout, used to send your payment receipt and to look up your license if you contact support.
  • License key and device identifier - when you activate Thomsen Shield, the app sends your license key and a hardware-derived identifier for that PC to our license server, so we can confirm the license is valid and bound to one device.
  • Payment information - handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Standard server logs - our hosting providers (Vercel, Supabase) log request metadata like IP address for security and abuse prevention, as most web infrastructure does.

2. What we don't collect

Thomsen Shield does not send us your network traffic, browsing history, the connections it observes, or the IPs it blocks. All detection and blocking logic runs locally on your machine. Nothing about your day-to-day usage of the app is transmitted anywhere.

3. Cookies

This site uses a cookie banner to ask for your consent before setting anything beyond strictly necessary cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for thomssen.dev.

4. How we use it

Solely to issue and validate your license, process your payment, prevent fraud (e.g. chargebacks, key sharing), and respond if you contact support. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell it to anyone.

5. Who we share it with

Only the services that make Thomsen Shield work:

  • Stripe - payment processing (their privacy policy)
  • Supabase - database hosting for license records
  • Vercel - hosting for our license server and this website

Each processes data under their own privacy policy and only as needed to provide their service to us.

6. Data retention

We keep license and purchase records for as long as your license is active, plus whatever period is required for tax and accounting records afterward.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing support@thomssen.dev. Note that deleting your account data will deactivate your license.

8. Security

Data in transit between the app and our server is encrypted (TLS). Your license token is cryptographically signed and stored encrypted on your own device.

9. Children

Thomsen Shield isn't directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
