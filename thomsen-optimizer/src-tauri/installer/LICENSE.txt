1. WHAT IT DOES
Thomsen Optimizer analyses your Windows PC and, only when you choose, changes
supported Windows settings (power plan, Game Mode, Game Bar capture, startup
entries, per-app graphics preference), closes applications you select and
deletes temporary files you select. Every settings change is recorded locally
and can be restored. Deleted files and closed applications cannot be restored
by Thomsen Optimizer.

2. NO GUARANTEES
Results depend on your hardware, drivers and games. Thomsen Optimizer does not
promise any FPS increase; use the built-in measurements to judge the effect on
your own system.

3. PRIVACY
All data (settings, change history, game library, measurements) stays on your
PC. Thomsen Optimizer contacts the internet only to check for application
updates on GitHub, and only if update checks are enabled.
