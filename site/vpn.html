Thomsen VPN

A Windows VPN for encrypting your traffic and connecting with one click. Details, screenshots and release information will be shared here and on Discord as the launch gets closer.

Get notified on Discord →All software
What is inside

Everything you need to connect.

Dashboard

Your connection status, how long you have been connected, the IP address websites see, and how much data you have transferred.

Connect

One button to connect and disconnect. The app only shows Connected once it has confirmed your traffic is really going through the VPN.

Server

Connect through our server in Finland. The app shows its status and is built so more locations can be added later.

Data used

See how much data you have used in your current billing period. There is no data limit.

License

Activate your Thomsen VPN license on your PC. One license works on one PC at a time, and you can move it to another PC any time.

Diagnostics

Check the background service, your license, the server, name lookup and your public IP in one click, and copy a report for support. Keys are never included.

Settings

Start with Windows, connect when the app starts, choose your server, and switch between dark and light themes and accent colours.
